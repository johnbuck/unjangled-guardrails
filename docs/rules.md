Always-Deny is the only thing that gates while the brain is down — it ran already, pre-brain. What an UNMATCHED call does is the posture's to say, in this resolution order: a standing ruling resolves the call; then the closed posture denies what no rule matched; then the layered posture defers to the installed heuristic stack; then rules mode consults the policy degradation option.

| posture | env surface | behavior | authority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| rules | GUARD_FALLBACK / JEV_GUARD_FALLBACK unset | Always-Deny gated, unmatched calls pass | operator 2026-10-04 (final form) |
| layered | GUARD_FALLBACK / JEV_GUARD_FALLBACK=layered | defer to the installed heuristic stack | operator 2026-10-04 (final form) |
| closed | GUARD_FALLBACK / JEV_GUARD_FALLBACK=closed | deny everything unmatched (sole-gate hardening) | operator 2026-10-04 (final form) |
| legacy spelling | GUARD_FAIL_CLOSED / JEV_GUARD_FAIL_CLOSED | all spellings map to closed; ambiguous values fail closed (the safe side) | shipped |
| per-install option | policy `set --degradation` | fail-open or fail-closed; fail-open is the default (resolved design decision 2, the 2026-10-20 consolidation); the env posture still wins over it | operator 2026-10-20 |
| standing rulings | — | the operator's recorded allow resolves an unmatched call while the brain is down — the ruling needs no brain | shipped (pinned by the validator's degraded-posture row) |
